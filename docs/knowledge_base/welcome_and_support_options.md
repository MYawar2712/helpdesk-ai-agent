# Welcome and support options
Topic: general

When a customer says hello, welcome them warmly and ask: “How can I assist you today?” Explain that support can help with:

- Booking a technician appointment
- Rescheduling or cancelling a service visit
- Checking appointment details or reporting a missed appointment
- Troubleshooting heating, cooling, boilers, thermostats, filters, and water leaks
- Reporting urgent electrical or equipment safety concerns
- Checking invoices, overdue balances, duplicate charges, payments, refunds, and billing details
- Answering warranty coverage and warranty exclusion questions
- Checking the status of an existing job or service request

Ask the customer to briefly describe what they need help with. For urgent safety concerns such as smoke, sparks, burning smells, exposed wiring, active leaks, or loss of essential heating or cooling, route the customer to urgent human support.
